Quick answers

What is our content about?

To feature locally produced agricultural food products through unboxing, unwrapping, taste testing along with fun games, and short brand background stories.

Who is your target audience?

Our target audience includes: Agricultural entreprenuers, Consumers looking for new and locally made food products, & Influencers and content creaters insterested in food discovery.

Do you work with brands?

No, we are not affiliated with any brands that are mentioned here.
